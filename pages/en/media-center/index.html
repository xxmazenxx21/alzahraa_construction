Behind the Archive

Documentation as part of the process, not an afterthought

A project record that holds up under a ministry's review starts on day one, not at handover.

  1. 01

    Milestone photography

    Every major construction stage — earthworks complete, sub-base laid, first asphalt course, structural pour — is photographed as part of the site's own quality record, not just for marketing.

  2. 02

    Periodic aerial surveys

    Scheduled drone passes track alignment, grading and overall progress across a corridor's full length, in a way ground photography alone can't.

  3. 03

    Safety and site walkthroughs recorded

    Toolbox talks, PPE checks and inspection walkthroughs are documented as part of the project's HSE trail, not only its photo archive.

  4. 04

    Handover archives

    Every completed project leaves behind a visual record — photos, footage and as-built references — that both the client and Al Zahraa can return to long after the ribbon is cut.
