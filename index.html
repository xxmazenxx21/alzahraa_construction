02 Who We Are

Three decades of building Egypt's connective tissue

Al Zahraa General Contracting, Import & Export Company was founded in 1995 and has operated without interruption in Egypt's heavy-civil sector ever since. Our specialisation has never drifted: roads, bridges and the earthworks, drainage and structural works that carry them.

That focus is deliberate. Road and bridge work is unforgiving — it is measured in compaction density, layer thickness, bearing capacity and the ability to keep a corridor moving while you rebuild it. Companies that try to do everything rarely do this well. We have spent thirty years doing one thing, in one country, under the supervision of the authorities that set its standards.

Today Al Zahraa works as a main contractor on national corridors, regional connections and municipal road programmes, executing under the technical supervision of the Ministry of Housing, the Ministry of Transport and the Armed Forces Engineering Authority.

10 Capability in the Field

From subgrade to surface course

A road is a stack of decisions. Subgrade preparation determines whether the corridor holds its level after the first heavy season. Sub-base and base course gradation determine load distribution. Asphalt temperature at laydown and roller pattern determine surface life. Al Zahraa controls that stack in-house — our own crews, our own plant, our own quality records — which is why our corridors are still carrying load years after handover.

  • Earthworks & subgrade preparation
  • Sub-base and base course
  • Asphalt binder and wearing courses
  • Bridge substructure & superstructure
  • Drainage, culverts and safety furniture
  • Road marking and signage
